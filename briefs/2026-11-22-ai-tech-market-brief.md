## 5. Foundations & Terms

### Concept Spotlight: War-Risk Insurance Premium

What it is: An extra charge that shipping companies pay to insure vessels sailing through dangerous waters (like the Strait of Hormuz). When tankers are attacked, these premiums skyrocket, adding directly to the cost of transporting oil.

Why it exists: Standard marine insurance doesn't cover war zones. War-risk insurance is a separate policy that covers damage from missiles, mines, piracy, and detention by hostile governments.

Concrete worked example: A tanker sailing from Saudi Arabia to Japan normally pays about 0.05% of the ship's value in insurance per voyage. If the ship is worth $100 million, that's $50,000. After tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, the war-risk premium can jump to 1% or more. That's $1 million per voyage. The shipping company passes this cost on to the oil buyer, adding roughly $1-2 per barrel to the price of oil.

How it shows up in the news: Headlines about "spiking insurance costs" for tankers. Analysts use it as a real-time gauge of geopolitical risk in oil markets.

Figure worth remembering: During the 2019 Hormuz tanker attacks, war-risk premiums for the region jumped 10x.

Common misconception: War-risk insurance is not just about the ship. It also covers the cargo. A single tanker can carry $50-100 million worth of crude oil. The total insured value (hull plus cargo) is what matters.

**Other Terms from Today's News**

- **Interim Final Rule (IFR):** A type of regulation in the US that takes effect immediately upon publication, without the usual public comment period first. The BIS used an IFR to close the TSMC loophole, showing the urgency of the policy.
- **Looped transformer:** A neural network architecture that feeds its output back into itself for a fixed number of steps, creating "recurrent depth." This allows a model to have more effective computation per token without increasing the number of parameters linearly. Inkling 2 Mini uses this.
- **Active parameters (in MoE):** In a Mixture-of-Experts model, the total parameters represent the entire "knowledge" of the model. The active parameters are the subset of experts that are actually turned on to process a single token. Inkling 2 Mini has 26.4B total but only 3.4B active, meaning it is very efficient to run.
- **Correction:** A decline of 10% to 19.99% from a recent peak. The S&P 500 just entered one. A bear market is a decline of 20% or more.
