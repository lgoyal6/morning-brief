## 5. Foundations & Terms

### Concept Spotlight: Active Parameters (in Mixture-of-Experts)

**What it is in plain words:** In a standard neural network, every parameter (weight) is used to process every input. In a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model, the network is divided into many smaller "expert" sub-networks. A "router" decides which few experts are best suited for each specific input. The **total parameters** is the sum of all experts' weights. The **active parameters** is the number of weights actually used for a single prediction.

**Why it exists:** MoE allows a model to have a vast amount of knowledge (high total parameters) while keeping the computational cost per query low (low active parameters). This makes inference faster and cheaper.

**Worked example with real numbers:** Alibaba's Qwen4-Max has **2.4 trillion total parameters** but only **71 billion active parameters** per token. That is a 97% reduction in compute per query. OVH's Olala mini has 7B total and 1B active.

**How it shows up in the news:** When a model is described as "MoE" or has a "7B / 1B active" split, this is the concept. It explains why a model can be both "huge" and "fast/cheap".

**Figure worth remembering:** Active parameters are typically 2-5% of total parameters in a large MoE model.

**Common misconception:** "Total parameters" is the headline number, but "active parameters" is what determines the cost and speed. A 2.4T model that activates only 71B is not 10x more expensive to run than a 240B dense model.

**Terms from today's news:**

- **Optical Transceiver:** A device that converts electrical signals into light signals (and vice versa) to transmit data over fiber optic cables. They are the backbone of data center networking. The US is planning to ban imports of Chinese-made ones, which would directly benefit US makers like Coherent (COHR) and Lumentum (LITE).

- **Series C:** A stage of venture capital funding, typically for companies that have a proven product and are scaling rapidly (e.g., expanding sales, entering new markets). It comes after Series A and B. Isomorphic Labs just raised a $5B Series C, one of the largest ever in AI biotech.

- **Proxy Play:** An investment strategy where you buy shares in one company because you believe it will benefit from the success of another company that you cannot easily invest in directly. For example, buying the Destiny Tech100 fund (DXYZ) as a proxy for the Anthropic IPO, since Anthropic is not yet publicly traded.

- **Export Control Panel:** An internal committee within a company (like Nvidia's newly formed one) that reviews and approves sales to foreign customers to ensure compliance with government export regulations, especially for sensitive technologies like AI chips.
