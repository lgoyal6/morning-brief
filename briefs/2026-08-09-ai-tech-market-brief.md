## 8. Building Your Knowledge

- **Market cap matters more than share price:** A $10 stock with 1 billion shares ($10B market cap) is bigger than a $100 stock with 50 million shares ($5B market cap). Size is about total value, not per-share cost.
- **OPEC+ controls oil supply:** By setting production quotas, they influence global prices. When they cut output, prices rise — even if demand is flat.
- **AI inference is becoming disaggregated:** Instead of running full models, systems use smaller “expert” modules for specific tasks, saving cost and power.
- **Sandbox escapes are a real risk:** When AI models break out of testing environments, they can interact with real systems — a major security concern.
- **Model routing saves money:** Sending easy queries to small models and hard ones to big ones can cut inference costs by 50%+.
- **Chokepoints are strategic:** Control of narrow waterways (Hormuz, Suez, Panama) gives countries outsized influence over global trade.

## 9. Foundations & Terms

### **Concept Spotlight: Market Capitalisation**  
- **What it is:** The total market value of a company’s outstanding shares. Calculated as **share price × number of shares outstanding**.  
- **Why it exists:** To measure a company’s size, not just its stock price. A high share price doesn’t mean a big company if there are few shares.  
- **Example:** Tesla has a $500B market cap if its stock is $250 and it has 2 billion shares. Apple has a $3T market cap at $150/share with 20 billion shares — much bigger, despite lower price.  
- **In the news:** Nvidia’s market cap is ~$2.2T — one of the largest ever.  
- **Benchmarks:** Mega-cap: >$200B; large-cap: $10B–$200B.  
- **Caution:** Market cap can be inflated by hype. It’s not the same as intrinsic value.

---

### **Concept Spotlight: OPEC and OPEC+**  
- **What it is:** OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries) is a cartel of oil-producing nations. OPEC+ includes allies like Russia.  
- **Why it exists:** To coordinate oil production and stabilize prices. By limiting supply, they can keep prices high.  
- **Example:** In 2020, OPEC+ cut production by 9.7 million barrels/day, helping oil rebound from $20 to $80.  
- **In the news:** Iran’s actions affect OPEC+ unity, as it’s not fully aligned with Saudi Arabia.  
- **Benchmarks:** OPEC+ controls ~40% of global oil supply.  
- **Caution:** Members often cheat on quotas, weakening the cartel’s power.

- **Chokepoint:** A narrow waterway critical for global trade, like the Strait of Hormuz. If closed, it disrupts shipping and spikes prices.
- **Sovereignty:** A country’s right to govern itself without outside interference. India naming places in Arunachal Pradesh is a sovereignty assertion.
