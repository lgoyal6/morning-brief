## 7. Analysis: Second-Order Effects

**1. Trump-Xi summit -> AI export controls discussion -> TSMC stock reaction**  
- **Facts:** Trump and Xi meet to discuss AI, chips, and Taiwan. U.S. export controls restrict advanced chip sales to China. TSMC makes most advanced chips.  
- **Inference:** If talks ease controls, TSMC could gain access to Chinese market. If they tighten, TSMC may face more restrictions.  
- **Weakest link:** TSMC already routes most China-bound chips through intermediaries. Direct sales are limited.  
- **Falsifying condition:** TSMC announces a new fab in China.  
- **Precedent:** 2020 U.S.-China trade war caused TSMC to build in Arizona.

**2. MiMo-V2.6 release -> open-weight adoption -> lower API revenue for Anthropic/OpenAI**  
- **Facts:** Xiaomi releases high-performance open model. It undercuts Opus 5.5 on price.  
- **Inference:** Enterprises may self-host MiMo instead of paying API fees. This reduces revenue for closed-model firms.  
- **Weakest link:** Open models require technical skill to deploy. Most companies lack in-house expertise.  
- **Falsifying condition:** Anthropic reports record API usage growth next quarter.  
- **Precedent:** 2023 Llama 2 release pressured OpenAI pricing.

**3. Data center power demand -> microgrid partnerships -> higher valuations for power tech firms**  
- **Facts:** AI data centers need reliable power. Zeo Energy and Ewyze partner on microgrids.  
- **Inference:** More such deals will follow, boosting firms in energy storage and grid management.  
- **Weakest link:** Utilities may block islanded grids to protect revenue.  
- **Falsifying condition:** FERC rules against off-grid data centers.  
- **Precedent:** 2022 Texas grid crisis spurred microgrid investments.

## 8. Building Your Knowledge

- **AI sovereignty is geopolitics:** Countries build domestic AI to reduce reliance on rivals. China’s MiMo is a sovereign stack response to U.S. export controls.
- **Capex guides demand:** Hyperscaler spending forecasts signal future AI growth. $1.3 trillion by 2027 means sustained demand for chips, power, and networking.
- **Open vs. closed models:** Open-weight models are free to use and modify. Closed models are APIs with usage fees. The trade-off is control vs. convenience.
- **Model routing cuts costs:** Send easy tasks to small models, hard ones to big ones. This can save 30–50% in inference costs.
- **Microgrids solve power gaps:** When the grid can’t support AI loads, companies build their own power systems with solar, batteries, and generators.

## 9. Foundations & Terms

### Concept Spotlight: Tax-Loss Harvesting (Markets/Investing)
- **What it is:** Selling losing investments to offset gains and reduce taxes.
- **Why it exists:** To let investors manage tax bills without changing long-term strategy.
- **Worked example:** You made $10,000 on Nvidia stock but lost $4,000 on bonds. Sell the bonds, use the $4,000 loss to reduce taxable gains to $6,000.
- **How it shows up:** CNBC advises bond investors to harvest losses now due to rising yields.
- **Benchmarks:** Must realize loss before year-end. Can’t repurchase the same asset within 30 days (wash-sale rule).
- **Caution:** Don’t sell good assets just for tax reasons. The investment thesis matters more.

### Concept Spotlight: Sovereignty (World Affairs)
- **What it is:** A country’s right to govern itself without outside interference.
- **Why it exists:** To prevent foreign domination and protect national interests.
- **Worked example:** Taiwan claims sovereignty, but China says it’s part of its territory. The U.S. supports Taiwan’s self-defense but doesn’t recognize it as a country.
- **How it shows up:** Xi will tell Trump Taiwan is a red line. Pezeshkian says Iran won’t let the U.S. dictate its nuclear program.
- **Benchmarks:** Sovereignty includes control over borders, military, and laws.
- **Caution:** Sovereignty claims don’t always match reality. Power often trumps principle.

- **Energy truce:** A temporary ceasefire focused on halting attacks on power plants, grids, or oil facilities. It aims to stabilize energy markets without resolving the war.
- **Open-weight model:** An AI model whose parameters are publicly available. Anyone can download, modify, or run it locally.
- **Capex (capital expenditure):** Spending on long-lived assets like factories, data centers, or equipment. It’s a sign of long-term confidence.
- **Context window:** The amount of text an AI can process in one go. MiMo-V2.6-Pro has a 1M-token window, allowing it to analyze entire books.
- **Hyperscaler:** A giant cloud provider like Amazon, Microsoft, or Google. They build and run massive data centers for AI.
